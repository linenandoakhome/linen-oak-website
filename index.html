A Note from Cristin

A beautiful home should never feel too precious to live in.

I believe the most memorable spaces are layered over time, filled with pieces that feel personal, useful, and quietly beautiful. Linen & Oak is where I share complete room ideas and favorite finds to help make that feeling more attainable.

The goal is not to copy a room exactly. It is to discover what inspires you, learn why the pieces work together, and create a home that feels distinctly yours.
